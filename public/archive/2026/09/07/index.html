Combined patterns

  • Agent evaluation should include the surrounding tools and permissions. Stronger task performance leaves those controls necessary.
  • A rights dispute can turn on records maintained years before an AI product appears. Creative teams should preserve contracts alongside their working files.
  • A model can perform well on one task and fail on a nearby task. Teams should define the intended action precisely before comparing alternatives.
  • Funding can support growth without making a service available. Procurement needs evidence at the point of delivery.
